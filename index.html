IT asset management for growing teams

A clear view of your assets, wherever your team works.

ShiftDesk helps small and mid-sized companies track every laptop, monitor and phone, know who has it, and get it back when someone leaves.

The problem

Spreadsheets were never meant to track hardware.

As a team grows, devices change hands faster than anyone can keep up with.

Lost track

Devices go missing after someone leaves, and nobody knows where they ended up.

No history

When there's a question about who had what, the answer lives in someone's memory.

Chasing updates

HR asks IT, IT asks the employee, and the status never reaches one place.

Product

Everything you need to manage your fleet.

Track what you own, know who has it and bring it back when it's time, all from one workspace that keeps growing with your team.

  1. Total assets, what's in use, what's in stock and what needs attention, all on one screen. Track allocation and net book value over time, and export a report whenever you need one.

  2. Every laptop, monitor, peripheral and phone in a single searchable catalog. Find any asset by tag, serial number or model, filter by category, and see its status and owner at a glance.

  3. Start a return when someone leaves, follow it from reminder to shipping label to inspection, and close it only when the equipment is received. HR and IT see the same status, so nobody has to ask.

  4. Assigned Devices, Custody Terms, Shipping, Maintenance, Audit Logs and Analytics are next.

How it works

From first device to final return in three steps.

ShiftDesk follows the life of every device, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Register your assets

Add devices one by one or in bulk, each with a tag, serial number and specs.

Assign to your team

Link every device to an employee and keep custody on record from day one.

Recover at exit

Start an offboarding request, track the return and close it with confidence.
